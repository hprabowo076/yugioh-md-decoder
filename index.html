This is a fan-made helper tool, unaffiliated with Konami / Master Duel. Card data may be inaccurate or out of sync with the game; treat suggestions as reference only and trust the in-game display. Also note: Master Duel excludes special collaboration cards (e.g. "Jikyuu Yakyuu Megumi Sisters"). If the candidate count doesn't line up and you spot a similar collab card, please report it via "Feedback" below; an off-by-one difference is fine to ignore and doesn't affect your judgment.
